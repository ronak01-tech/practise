Ada Lovelace

First software Developer

Ada Lovelace (born Augusta Ada Byron on December 10, 1815) was an English mathematician and writer widely celebrated as the world's first computer programmer.

Major Achievements • The First Computer Program (Note G): While translating an article on Charles Babbage's mechanical computer, the Analytical Engine, written by Luigi Menabrea, Lovelace added extensive explanatory notes that were three times longer than the original text. In Note G, she detailed a step-by-step algorithm to calculate Bernoulli numbers, making it the first published computer program in history
